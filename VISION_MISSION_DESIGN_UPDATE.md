# Vision & Mission Section - Design Update

Applied the enhanced design concept from "Why Choose FranklinOne" section to the Vision & Mission section.

### 1. **Background & Atmosphere**
- ✅ Changed to dark gradient background: `from-gray-900 via-gray-800 to-gray-900`
- ✅ Added animated tech grid pattern with moving lines
- ✅ Added 20 floating particles with complex animations (scale, rotate, opacity)

### 2. **Section Header**
- ✅ Added animated badge with pulsing dot indicator

### 3. **Content Layout**
- ✅ Changed from card-based to open layout design
- ✅ Added numbered indicators with circular icons

- ✅ Improved typography and spacing
- ✅ Two-row grid layout (Vision top, Mission bottom)

### 4. **Image Treatment**
- ✅ Larger image containers (h-80)
- ✅ Added animated rotating rings around images

- ✅ Enhanced with backdrop blur and stronger borders
